## Part B: Additional terms for legal entities

Part B applies when an individual contributes on behalf of a company, public
body or other legal entity ("Entity"), for example as part of their employment.

1. The individual accepting this Agreement for the Entity confirms they are
   authorised to bind it.
2. The Entity grants the licences in Part A for every Contribution submitted
   by its designated employees or contractors.
3. The Entity sends info@datadrivenconstruction.io a list of the GitHub accounts
   authorised to contribute on its behalf and keeps that list up to date.
   Contributions from an account are covered from the date it is added until
   the date it is removed.
4. Each designated individual also signs Part A through the CLA bot, so that
   every pull request is checked.
5. The Entity is not obliged to provide support for its Contributions.

To sign for an Entity, e-mail info@datadrivenconstruction.io with the Entity's
legal name, address, signatory name and title, and the list of GitHub accounts.
You will receive a countersigned copy.

## How to sign

1. Open a pull request.
2. The CLA Assistant bot comments with a link to this file.
3. Read the Agreement, then post this comment on the pull request:

   > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

4. The bot records Your GitHub username, user ID, the pull request and the
   timestamp in `signatures/cla.json` on the `cla-signatures` branch. You sign
   once; later pull requests pass automatically.
