# Roadmap

Near-term work, in no fixed order. Plans change; open an issue to discuss priorities.

- **Business onboarding wizard.** A guided first run: company data, chart of accounts, tax settings, bank account and the first documents.
- **PDF templates.** Selectable and editable layouts for quotes, invoices and reminders.
- **Translations.** Completing the 45 interface locale bundles for the business modules.
- **Public demo.** A hosted, regularly reset demo instance to try without installing.
- **Payment links.** A pay-online link on invoices through a payment provider, matched back to the invoice when paid.

Contributions to any of these are welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
